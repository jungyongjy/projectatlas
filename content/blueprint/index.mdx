The Blueprint defines **why** Atlas exists and **how** training decisions are made.

It is not a workout programme. It is the operating philosophy that governs every recommendation throughout the handbook.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Philosophy**: The core beliefs underpinning the Atlas training system
- **Goals**: Current training objectives and long-term performance targets
- **Principles**: The five foundational principles for all programming decisions
- **Weekly Schedule**: The fixed weekly structure balancing gym, running, and rest

<InfoBox title="How to use the Blueprint">
Start with Philosophy to understand the reasoning behind every recommendation, then explore Goals and Principles to see how they translate into action.
</InfoBox>

<TipBox>
The Blueprint is designed to be read once and referenced occasionally. It does not change week to week.
</TipBox>
