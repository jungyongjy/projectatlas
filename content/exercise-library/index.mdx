## Transfer Ratings

Each exercise is rated 1–5 for transfer to running:

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ★★★★★ | Direct, significant transfer |
| ★★★★☆ | Strong transfer |
| ★★★☆☆ | Moderate transfer |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Indirect transfer |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | Minimal direct transfer |

<InfoBox title="Transfer is not value">
A low transfer rating does not mean an exercise is low-value. Preacher Curls have minimal running transfer but high value for arm development, which supports physique goals. Transfer ratings describe how directly an exercise improves running performance, not how important the exercise is overall.
</InfoBox>
