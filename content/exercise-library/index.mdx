## Transfer Ratings

Each exercise is rated 1–5 for transfer to three domains:

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ★★★★★ | Direct, significant transfer |
| ★★★★☆ | Strong transfer |
| ★★★☆☆ | Moderate transfer |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Indirect transfer |
| ★☆☆☆☆ | Minimal direct transfer |

<InfoBox title="Transfer is not value">
A low transfer rating does not mean an exercise is low-value. Preacher Curls have minimal running transfer but high value for arm development — which supports physique goals. Transfer ratings describe how directly an exercise improves a specific domain, not how important the exercise is overall.
</InfoBox>
