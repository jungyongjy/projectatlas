The Cable Triceps Extension is the primary triceps isolation exercise in the Atlas programme. Cable resistance provides constant tension throughout the range of motion — a significant advantage over free-weight triceps exercises where tension varies with leverage. It is included in both Template A and Template B, providing approximately six direct sets per week.

## Purpose

Triceps brachii hypertrophy. The triceps comprise approximately two-thirds of upper arm mass — triceps development is more important for arm size than biceps development. Constant tension from the cable is ideal for isolation work.

## Muscles

| Role | Muscles |
|------|---------|
| **Primary** | Triceps brachii (all three heads) |
| **Secondary** | Anconeus (small elbow extensor) |

## Execution

1. Attach a rope or straight bar to a high cable pulley
2. Grip the attachment and position the upper arms vertically alongside the ears
3. Extend the elbows until the arms are straight, squeezing the triceps at full extension
4. Control the return to the starting position — allow the elbows to flex fully

| Parameter | Guideline |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 10–15 |
| **Attachment** | Rope (allows neutral grip and greater range) or straight bar |
| **Upper Arms** | Fixed position alongside the head — only the forearms should move |

## Common Mistakes

### Moving the Upper Arms

Allowing the elbows to drift forward or flare outward. The upper arms should remain fixed — all movement occurs at the elbow joint. If the elbows are moving, the lats and shoulders are contributing, reducing triceps stimulus.

### Using Momentum

Swinging the weight down using body movement rather than controlling the eccentric with the triceps. Each rep should be deliberate — a controlled eccentric is at least as important as the concentric.

### Partial Range of Motion

Not fully extending the elbows (skipping peak contraction) or not fully flexing (skipping the stretch). Full range of motion ensures all three triceps heads are trained through their full length.

<CoachNote>
Cable Triceps Extensions are included in both templates — this is intentional. The triceps are a large muscle group that recovers relatively quickly and benefits from higher frequency. Six direct sets per week (3 per session × 2 sessions) provides adequate volume for hypertrophy without excessive fatigue.
</CoachNote>
