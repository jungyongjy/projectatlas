## Overview

The Rear Delt Reverse Fly isolates the rear deltoids and upper back musculature, areas that are often underdeveloped relative to the anterior shoulder. It is included for shoulder health and postural balance rather than performance. Developed rear delts create a complete shoulder appearance and counterbalance the internal rotation bias of pressing exercises and desk work.

## Purpose

Posterior deltoid, rhomboid, and external rotator development. Shoulder health through balanced anterior-posterior development. Postural support.

## Execution

1. Adjust the seat and handles so the arms extend at shoulder height
2. Grip the handles with palms facing each other (neutral) or palms facing down (pronated)
3. Pull the handles back by driving the elbows rearward, squeezing the shoulder blades together
4. Control the return; do not let the weight stack touch down between reps

| Parameter | Guideline |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Sets × Reps** | 2 × 10 |
| **Range** | Full: arms forward to shoulder blades fully retracted |
| **Tempo** | 1–2 second squeeze at peak contraction |

## Common Mistakes

### Using Too Much Weight

The rear delts are small muscles. Excessive weight forces the larger back muscles (lats, traps) to take over, missing the specific rear delt stimulus. Use a weight that allows a clear rear delt contraction on every rep.

### Rushing Reps

Bouncing through reps without a clear contraction at the end range. Pause briefly at peak retraction to ensure the rear delts (not momentum) are performing the work.

### Shrugging

Allowing the traps to elevate and take over the movement. Keep the shoulders depressed and let the rear delts drive the retraction.

## Running Transfer

**Transfer: ★★☆☆☆.** The Rear Delt Reverse Fly does not directly improve running performance. Its value is preventive: balanced shoulder development supports consistent training across all domains. Shoulder issues are among the most common training-disrupting injuries, and this exercise is inexpensive insurance.

## Alternatives

| Exercise | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Cable Reverse Fly** | Constant tension through the range; easier to adjust load |
| **Bent-Over Dumbbell Reverse Fly** | Free-weight alternative; greater stabiliser demand |
| **Face Pull** | Similar posterior shoulder emphasis with an external rotation component |

<CoachNote>
The Rear Delt Reverse Fly does not make you faster or stronger. It keeps your shoulders healthy, which enables you to train consistently. Its value is preventive. Perform it with light weight and strict form, feeling the rear delts do the work rather than chasing load.
</CoachNote>

<ScientificNote>
Strength training for the external rotators and posterior shoulder is associated with reduced risk of shoulder impingement and improved scapular control in overhead athletes. Because pressing volume in most programmes far exceeds pulling volume, targeted posterior shoulder work helps restore muscular balance around the shoulder joint.
</ScientificNote>
