The Reverse Pec Deck isolates the rear deltoids and upper back musculature — areas that are often underdeveloped relative to the anterior shoulder. It is included for shoulder health and postural balance, not performance. Developed rear delts create a complete shoulder appearance and counterbalance the internal rotation bias of pressing exercises and desk work.

## Purpose

Posterior deltoid, rhomboid, and external rotator development. Shoulder health through balanced anterior-posterior development. Postural support.

## Muscles

| Role | Muscles |
|------|---------|
| **Primary** | Posterior deltoid, rhomboids, middle trapezius |
| **Secondary** | Infraspinatus, teres minor (external rotators) |

## Execution

1. Adjust the seat and handles so the arms extend at shoulder height
2. Grip the handles with palms facing each other (neutral) or palms facing down (pronated)
3. Pull the handles back by driving the elbows rearward, squeezing the shoulder blades together
4. Control the return — do not let the weight stack touch down between reps

| Parameter | Guideline |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 10–15 |
| **Range** | Full — arms forward to shoulder blades fully retracted |
| **Tempo** | 1–2 second squeeze at peak contraction |

<InfoBox title="Shoulder health as performance enabler">
The Reverse Pec Deck does not make you faster or stronger. It keeps your shoulders healthy — which enables you to train consistently. Its value is preventive. Shoulder issues are among the most common training-disrupting injuries. This exercise is inexpensive insurance.
</InfoBox>

## Common Mistakes

### Using Too Much Weight

The rear delts are small muscles. Excessive weight forces the larger back muscles (lats, traps) to take over — missing the specific rear delt stimulus. Use a weight that allows a clear rear delt contraction on every rep.

### Rushing Reps

Bouncing through reps without a clear contraction at the end range. Pause briefly at peak retraction to ensure the rear delts — not momentum — are performing the work.
