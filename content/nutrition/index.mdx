Nutrition in Atlas is treated as **fuel for training**, not a separate optimisation project. Simple, consistent habits that support performance and recovery, without tracking apps, macro calculators, or meal plans.

## Philosophy

Atlas nutrition follows four principles:

1. **Adequate before optimal**: Hit the minimums consistently before worrying about optimisation
2. **Protein first**: Protein is the most important macronutrient for a hybrid athlete
3. **Hydration is foundational**: Dehydration degrades both running and lifting performance
4. **Supplements supplement**: They fill gaps; they do not replace food

<InfoBox title="No tracking required">
Atlas does not prescribe calorie counting or macro tracking. These tools have their place, but the goal is to develop nutritional intuition, the ability to eat appropriately without external measurement. The recommendations below are designed to work without an app.
</InfoBox>

## Quick Reference

| Priority | Daily Target |
|----------|-------------|
| **Protein** | 140–160 g |
| **Calories** | ~2,000–2,500 kcal |
| **Hydration** | 2–3 L water |
| **Supplements** | Whey protein, creatine |

<CoachNote>
Nutrition is the most overcomplicated aspect of fitness. Eat enough protein. Drink enough water. Eat mostly whole foods. Do not overthink the rest. The athlete who follows these four rules consistently for years will outperform the athlete who optimises every micronutrient but cannot sustain the effort.
</CoachNote>
