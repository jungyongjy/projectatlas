This page is the single source of truth for the current Atlas training programme. Other handbook pages reference this page rather than duplicating programme details, so the programme and its documentation can never drift apart.

The programme is intentionally fixed. It changes slowly, deliberately, and only when the evidence or the athlete changes. When it does change, this page changes first, and every page that references it updates automatically.

## The Weekly Structure

The week alternates gym and running with one complete rest day.

| Day | Session |
|-----|---------|
| **Monday** | Full Body A or B |
| **Tuesday** | Hard Run |
| **Wednesday** | Full Body A or B |
| **Thursday** | Easy Zone 2 Run |
| **Friday** | Full Body A or B |
| **Saturday** | Long Run |
| **Sunday** | Complete Rest |

Gym sessions rotate between the two templates below. Each gym session targets approximately 30 minutes, built from supersets with minimal rest.

<InfoBox title="Supersets explained">
A superset pairs two exercises performed back-to-back with minimal rest between them. You rest after completing both exercises. This cuts session time nearly in half without compromising quality: the rest period for one muscle group becomes the work period for another.
</InfoBox>

## Calf Raises

Standing or seated calf raises remain part of the programme but are intentionally excluded from the logged routine. They rotate based on fatigue: the specific variation and loading are adjusted by how the calves feel on the day, so they are performed outside the fixed templates rather than prescribed within them.

<CoachNote>
The order of exercises within each template is intentional. Compound movements come first, when you are fresh. Isolation work follows. Core and posterior chain work at the end ensures your trunk is not pre-fatigued for compound lifts that require stability.
</CoachNote>

## Execution Guidelines

### Rest Periods
- **Supersets**: Rest ~60 seconds after completing both exercises
- **Straight sets** (Bulgarian Split Squat, Romanian Deadlift): Rest 60–90 seconds

### Tempo
- **Concentric** (lifting): Controlled, explosive intent
- **Eccentric** (lowering): 2–3 seconds, controlled

### Range of Motion
- Full range of motion on every exercise unless a specific limitation prevents it
- Partial reps are not programmed: they reduce stimulus without reducing fatigue

### Warm-Up Sets
- First exercise of each session: 1–2 light warm-up sets before working sets
- Subsequent exercises: No additional warm-up needed (muscles are already warm)

## Programme Integrity

This programme is the canonical source. Do not modify the exercise selection, ordering, supersets, sets, or reps without updating this page first. Other handbook pages, including the [Weekly Schedule](/blueprint/weekly-schedule), the [Weekly Programme](/strength/weekly-program), and the [Exercise Library](/exercise-library), reference this page as the single source of truth.

<TipBox>
If you have extra time, do not add exercises, improve the quality of the ones already programmed. Slower eccentrics, better mind-muscle connection, and more precise execution produce better results than additional volume performed with mediocre quality.
</TipBox>
