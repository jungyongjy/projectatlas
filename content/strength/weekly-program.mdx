The strength programme uses two rotating full-body templates, Full Body A and Full Body B, performed three times per week. This structure maximises exercise frequency while maintaining manageable session duration.

## The Rotation

| Week | Monday | Wednesday | Saturday |
|------|--------|-----------|----------|
| Week 1 | A | B | A |
| Week 2 | B | A | B |
| Week 3 | A | B | A |

This ensures each template is performed 4–5 times per month, adequate frequency for strength and hypertrophy maintenance while providing sufficient variation.

## Session Structure

Every gym session follows the same structure:

1. **Warm-Up**: 2–3 minutes dynamic movement; warm-up sets for the first exercise
2. **Main Work**: Superset pairs performed with ~60 seconds rest between supersets
3. **Core and Posterior**: Superset pairs at the end of the session
4. **Calf Raises**: Performed outside the logged routine (see below)

<InfoBox title="Supersets explained">
A superset pairs two exercises performed back-to-back with minimal rest between them. You rest after completing both exercises. This cuts session time nearly in half without compromising quality: the rest period for one muscle group becomes the work period for another.
</InfoBox>

## Calf Raises

Standing or seated calf raises remain part of the programme but are intentionally excluded from the logged routine in the application. They rotate based on fatigue: the specific variation and loading are adjusted by how the calves feel on the day, so they are performed outside the fixed template rather than prescribed within it.

## Execution Guidelines

### Rest Periods
- **Supersets**: Rest ~60 seconds after completing both exercises
- **Straight sets** (Bulgarian Split Squat, Romanian Deadlift): Rest 60–90 seconds

### Tempo
- **Concentric** (lifting): Controlled, explosive intent
- **Eccentric** (lowering): 2–3 seconds, controlled

### Range of Motion
- Full range of motion on every exercise unless a specific limitation prevents it
- Partial reps are not programmed: they reduce stimulus without reducing fatigue

### Warm-Up Sets
- First exercise of each session: 1–2 light warm-up sets before working sets
- Subsequent exercises: No additional warm-up needed (muscles are already warm)

<CoachNote>
The order of exercises within each template is intentional. Compound movements come first (when you are fresh). Isolation work follows. Core and posterior chain work at the end ensures your trunk is not pre-fatigued for compound lifts that require stability.
</CoachNote>

<TipBox>
If you have extra time, do not add exercises, improve the quality of the ones already programmed. Slower eccentrics, better mind-muscle connection, and more precise execution produce better results than additional volume performed with mediocre quality.
</TipBox>
