Training is straightforward when everything goes according to plan. The value of a decision framework is in handling the moments when it does not.

The Decision Engine provides practical, evidence-informed guidance for common training disruptions. Each decision tree follows the same structure: assess the situation, evaluate the options, and receive a recommendation with supporting reasoning.

## How to Use

1. **Find the scenario** that matches your situation
2. **Answer the questions honestly**: the recommendation is only as good as your self-assessment
3. **Follow the recommendation**: resist the impulse to override it with emotion

## The Meta-Decision

When none of the specific scenarios above apply, fall back to the Decision Framework from the Blueprint Principles:

1. **Does it move me toward my long-term identity?** → If no, stop
2. **Does it provide a high return on investment?** → Reconsider if the ratio is poor
3. **Can I recover from it?** → Recovery capacity is finite
4. **Will it interfere with more important training?** → Running takes priority
5. **Can I realistically continue doing this next year?** → Sustainability is the ultimate filter

<CoachNote>
The quality of your decisions determines the quality of your training. The quality of your training determines the quality of your results. Decision-making is a skill, develop it. The Decision Engine exists to reduce the number of decisions you need to make from scratch. Use it.
</CoachNote>
