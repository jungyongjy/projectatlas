The Scientific Appendix provides evidence summaries for the key topics underpinning the Atlas training system. Each summary explains what the research shows, why it matters for programming decisions, and where the evidence is strong versus preliminary.

This is not a comprehensive literature review. It is a practical reference, the scientific rationale behind the recommendations, collected in one place.

<InfoBox title="Evidence-informed, not evidence-bound">
Science guides Atlas programming, it does not dictate it. Research describes averages and probabilities. Coaching applies those probabilities to an individual. When evidence and individual response conflict, individual response takes priority, documented and reviewed, not ignored.
</InfoBox>
